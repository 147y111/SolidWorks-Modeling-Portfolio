# SolidWorks Modeling Portfolio

**Software:** SOLIDWORKS · **Focus:** Part Modeling and Mechanical Geometry

This portfolio documents more than fifty SOLIDWORKS modeling exercises completed as a sustained study of mechanical part design. The collection includes shafts, brackets, supports, valve and pump bodies, cylinders, housings, covers, bases, springs, levers, connecting parts, and other mechanical components. All 53 original `.SLDPRT` files are included in the [`models`](models) folder.

The exercises developed my ability to interpret mechanical forms, plan an efficient feature sequence, constrain sketches, and translate dimensions into editable parametric models. Rather than focusing on a single assembly, the collection covers a wide range of geometry and manufacturing-style features, from rotational parts and mounting supports to complex cast-style housings with internal openings and repeated details.

Representative models include:

- spline shaft and small shaft
- bearing housing and sleeve support
- brackets, supports, bases, and guide blocks
- valve body, valve cover, and pump bodies
- clamping cylinder and cylinder body
- transmission and worm-gear housings
- compression spring and spring sleeve
- fork, lever, connecting rod, and flange adapter

Across the collection, I practiced sketch constraints, extruded and revolved features, cuts, holes, fillets, chamfers, shells, patterns, reference geometry, and feature-tree organization. The models also required choosing suitable reference planes and breaking complex parts into manageable modeling steps.

The exercises were completed over an extended learning period and are presented as evidence of consistent CAD practice rather than as a single finished product. Several filenames remain in Chinese because they preserve the names used during the original modeling work.

GitHub cannot render native SOLIDWORKS files directly in the browser. Download a `.SLDPRT` file from the `models` folder and open it in a compatible version of SOLIDWORKS to inspect its feature tree and editable geometry.
